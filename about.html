A Simple Idea from a Real Living Room

Pik-N-Clik was invented by Chuku Lee after years of dealing with the frustration of too many remotes in his own home. Like millions of people, he found himself constantly picking up the wrong remote or spending precious minutes searching for the one he needed — only to realize it had slipped between the cushions again.

The frustration was familiar and universal. TVs, cable boxes, streaming sticks, soundbars — modern home entertainment setups can easily come with three, four, or even five different remote controls. Keeping track of all of them felt impossible.

A Rubber Band and a Better Idea

One evening, after trying to keep two remotes together with a rubber band, Chuku had a clear realization: this problem had a simple solution. People didn't need a complicated organizer or a massive caddy sitting on the coffee table. They needed something handheld, lightweight, and easy to carry from room to room — something that just worked.

That moment of clarity became Pik-N-Clik. Not a tech product. Not a subscription service. Just a well-made, practical holder that keeps your remotes together, right where you need them.

"We all have a fistful of remotes lying around the house. Organizing them in one place helps keep us sane. Bring on the popcorn."
— Chuku Lee, Inventor & Founder

Built for Real People

Chuku designed Pik-N-Clik with real households in mind. Seniors who struggle with too many devices. Families with children who move remotes from room to room. Hotel managers and Airbnb hosts who want guests to always find what they need. Office managers and waiting rooms where multiple people use the same entertainment setup.

Pik-N-Clik is not complicated. It does not require an app, a Wi-Fi connection, or a manual. You peel the dots, stick them on your remotes, click them onto the holder, and you're done. Everything stays together.

Made in the USA

From the beginning, Chuku was committed to making Pik-N-Clik in the United States. He wanted the product to reflect the same straightforward, no-nonsense values that inspired it: quality you can trust, at a price that makes sense.

Pik-N-Clik is proudly manufactured in the USA and is currently patent pending.

The Right Solution for an Everyday Problem

Sometimes the best inventions are the simplest ones. Pik-N-Clik is not a complex gadget. It is a small, affordable, useful product that solves a problem that every household has dealt with at one point or another.

If you have ever lost the remote, this product is for you.
