What Is Pik-N-Clik?

A Smarter Way to
Keep Remotes Together

Pik-N-Clik is a simple remote-control holder that keeps your remotes together in one convenient place. Unlike a bulky remote-control caddy, Pik-N-Clik is handheld, lightweight, and easy to carry from room to room.

It is large enough that it's hard to lose, yet compact enough to fit on a coffee table, bookshelf, side table, or bedside stand. Just attach, click, and you're done.

Simple Setup

How It Works

Four easy steps and you're done. No tools. No hassle. Just remotes that stay put.

1
⭐

Peel the Dots

Peel the white adhesive dots from the Pik-N-Clik holder. They're pre-cut and ready to go.

2
🧹

Prep Your Remote

Wipe the back of your remote clean, then remove the adhesive backing and apply the dots to the remote.

3
📷

Click It On

Attach the remote to the Pik-N-Clik holder. It clicks securely into place — simple and satisfying.

4
🎉

Add Up to 3

Repeat with up to three remotes. Now they're all in one place, exactly where they belong.

Our Story

Invented by a Real Person
Solving a Real Problem

Pik-N-Clik was invented by Chuku Lee after years of dealing with the frustration of too many remotes in his own home. Like many people, he found himself constantly picking up the wrong remote or searching for the one he needed.

One day, after trying to keep remotes together with rubber bands, he realized there had to be a better way. That simple idea became Pik-N-Clik — a product born not in a lab, but in a living room, from a problem everyone knows.

"We all have a fistful of remotes lying around the house. Organizing them in one place helps keep us sane. Bring on the popcorn."
